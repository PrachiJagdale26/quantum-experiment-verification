Deterministic GHZ State Preparation in a Trapped Ion Register

SYNTHETIC SAMPLE PAPER: written to demonstrate the research-gap pipeline. Not a real publication.

Abstract
We demonstrate deterministic preparation of the three-qubit Greenberger-Horne-Zeilinger state in a linear chain of trapped ion qubits. Cascaded entangling gates produce the expected tripartite correlations with high contrast.

Methodology
Three 171Yb+ ions are confined in a linear Paul trap. Entangling operations use Molmer-Sorensen interactions compiled to CNOT-equivalent gates. Populations are read out with state-dependent fluorescence.

Experiment 1: Three-qubit GHZ state on a 3-qubit register
We prepare the GHZ state on 3 qubits. H gate on qubit 0, then CX gate on qubits 0, 1, then CX gate on qubits 1, 2.
We measured P(000) = 0.5 and P(111) = 0.5, with a parity contrast of 0.93.

Results
We observed a GHZ fidelity of 93% for the trapped ion chain. We demonstrate that cascaded entangling gates scale linearly in depth with register size.

Limitations
We only demonstrate GHZ preparation up to 3 qubits; beyond 3 qubits the motional-mode crosstalk was not characterised. The protocol is restricted to trapped ion hardware.

Open Questions
It remains open whether comparable GHZ fidelities can be reached on superconducting transmon devices. It is unknown whether the linear depth scaling holds for 4 or more qubits.

A Hardware-Efficient VQE Ansatz for Molecular Hydrogen on Trapped Ions

SYNTHETIC SAMPLE PAPER: written to demonstrate the research-gap pipeline. Not a real publication.

Abstract
We introduce a hardware-efficient Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) ansatz and use it to estimate the ground-state energy of molecular hydrogen on a trapped ion processor. The reference state is prepared with single-qubit flips followed by an entangling layer.

Methodology
A two-ion chain encodes the minimal H2 Hamiltonian in the parity mapping. The classical optimiser is COBYLA, and the VQE loop is orchestrated with Qiskit.

Experiment 1: Reference-state preparation on a 2-qubit register
The Hartree-Fock reference is prepared on 2 qubits. X gate on qubit 0, then CX gate on qubits 0, 1.
We measured P(11) = 1.0 for the reference state before the variational layer.

Results
We achieved chemical accuracy (error below 1.6 mHa) at the equilibrium bond length. Our main contribution is an ansatz with half the two-qubit gate count of the unitary coupled-cluster baseline.

Limitations
All runs are restricted to trapped ion hardware with all-to-all connectivity. The ansatz does not account for limited connectivity on nearest-neighbour architectures.

Future Work
Benchmarking the same VQE ansatz on neutral atom arrays and superconducting transmon devices will be explored in future work.
